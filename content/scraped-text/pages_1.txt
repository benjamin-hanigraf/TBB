Service Description
Hello! The class has been constructed to help a beginner understand watercolour as a medium.
·	Introduction to watercolour as a medium
·	Techniques involved in watercolour
·	Understanding watercolour and ink

All the materials will be provided in the class and you can take back the basic kit.
