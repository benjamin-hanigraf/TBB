Service Description
In this beginner friendly course you will learn the basics of calligraphy, starting from basic strokes, practice drills, letter formation (uppercase and lowercase), numbers and connecting words.
You will be familiar with the techniques involved in Modern calligraphy.
The classes are structured to cover the following :
Section 1 - Introduction and basics of Modern Dip pen calligraphy - Materials required and maintenance - Understanding basic strokes
Section  2 - Introduction to letter formation - Understanding Lowercase alphabets
Section 3- Understanding Uppercase alphabets -  Numbers - Word formations - Understanding guidelines
Section 4 - Exploring different Modern Calligraphy Styles
The kits contain :
You will receive : Oblique holder - Speedball Calligraphy Ink - Leonardt 40 - Calligraphy Practice sheets - Alphabet sheet
